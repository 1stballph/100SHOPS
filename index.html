Questions

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can order as a guest and pay cash. Sign up (it's free) if you want to earn points, pay with your wallet, and set a food budget.

How much does it cost?

Each order has a fixed ₱50 service charge, plus a delivery fee if you choose delivery. Part of every service charge goes into the community pool that pays for your points.

Where can I use my points?

At any 100 - SHOPS store, on orders placed in the app. Points can't be used for walk-in purchases. You can order in the app and choose pick up instead.

Do points expire?

Points last 12 months. Your oldest points are used first.

How do I install the app?

Open the shop page on your phone. On Android, tap Install when it appears. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
